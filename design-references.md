# Design references

Reviewed on 29 September 2026 for layout and information organization, not for reuse of scientific content or imagery.

- [Satija Lab](https://satijalab.org/): concise laboratory introduction and straightforward academic navigation.
- [Azizi Lab](https://www.azizilab.com/): a research-led description that connects biological questions with computational methods.
- [Human Cell Atlas Data Portal](https://data.humancellatlas.org/): a clear distinction between a research resource and its individual dataset collections.

Landau Lab was initially inspected based on broad keyword overlap. It is not a research affiliation or a primary design reference; it was set aside after user feedback.

## Applied to Shi Lab

- Make the lab's research direction visible immediately.
- Keep research, datasets, images, publications, and lab information distinguishable.
- Apply the selected academic design A: white backgrounds, navy text, restrained blue accents, and a consistent sans-serif type scale.
- Pair a concise introduction with a real, credited lung adenocarcinoma micrograph; keep it separate from the sequencing-validated atlas collection.
- Use a shared header, footer, spacing system, and responsive behavior across all pages.
- Add once-only scroll reveals, restrained hover feedback, animated mobile navigation, and smooth dataset-filter changes; respect reduced-motion preferences and preserve visibility without JavaScript.
- Keep published research metadata intact and label resources that are still in preparation.
- Avoid decorative cell icons, simulated scientific data, paper-figure collages, and unrelated stock photographs.

The home page intentionally does not include a representative-studies section. Individual publications remain on the publications page. No images, logos, affiliation details, or scientific text have been copied from the reference websites.
